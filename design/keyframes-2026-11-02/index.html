How a setup happens

Nothing is published after the fact. A setup is written before the open, waits for its price, and is graded when it closes.

  1. Overnight

    The scanner ranks 5,400+ stocks by options flow, relative volume and momentum, and keeps the names whose flow has broken from their own baseline.

  2. Before the open

    The AI writes each finalist up as a setup: the price that has to hold, the target, the time window and the reasoning, in plain English.

  3. When the price agrees

    The entry rules trigger and an alert goes out. Until then the setup waits, and nothing is published after the fact.

  4. After it closes

    Every setup is graded against real prices, hit or miss, and the result joins the public record.
